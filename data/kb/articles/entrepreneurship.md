Every company you know started with someone who had an idea and decided to try it. That person is called an **entrepreneur**.

## What entrepreneurs do

An entrepreneur starts a business. Usually they see a problem or a need and think of a way to solve it. Then they turn the idea into something people can buy.

A kid who notices that neighbors are too busy to walk their dogs, and starts a dog-walking service, is being an entrepreneur.

## Ideas come from problems

Good business ideas often start with a question like:

- What is annoying, slow, or hard for people?
- What do people wish they had?
- What am I good at that others need?

## Taking a risk

Starting a business means taking a risk. You might spend money on supplies and time on planning, and it might not work. Entrepreneurs accept that risk because the business might succeed. If it does, they can earn a profit and help people.

Many famous businesses failed several times before they worked. Each try teaches something.

## Skills entrepreneurs use

- **Planning:** working out costs, prices, and steps
- **Talking with people:** finding customers and listening to them
- **Problem solving:** fixing things when they go wrong
- **Not giving up:** trying again after a setback

## Not just about money

Some entrepreneurs start businesses mainly to help others or their community. These are sometimes called social entrepreneurs. Making a difference can be the main reward.

## Try it

Think of a small problem at home, at school, or in your neighborhood. Write down one business idea that could help. What would it cost to start? Who would your customers be? What price could you charge?
