Banks use their own words. Here are some you will hear a lot, explained simply.

## Deposit

A **deposit** is money you put into your account. Adding birthday money to a savings account is a deposit.

## Withdrawal

A **withdrawal** is money you take out of your account, like cash from an ATM or a payment from a debit card.

## Balance

Your **balance** is how much money is in your account right now. Deposits make it go up, and withdrawals make it go down.

## Overdraft

An **overdraft** happens when you spend more than your balance, so the account goes below zero. Banks often charge a fee for it, so it is something to avoid.

## Routing number

A **routing number** is a nine-digit number that identifies a bank. It is used with an account number to send money, like when a paycheck is deposited.

## Account number

Your **account number** identifies your own account at the bank. Keep it private, like a password.

## ATM

An **ATM**, or automated teller machine, lets you take out cash or check your balance with a card and a secret PIN.

## Statement

A **statement** is a list of everything that happened in your account over a month: deposits, withdrawals, fees, and interest.
