Saving is easier when you know what you are saving for. A savings goal is a clear target. It has a name, a price, and a date.

## Make it clear

"I want to save money" is a wish. "I want to save $60 for a game in 8 weeks" is a goal. The second one tells you exactly what to do.

A good goal answers three questions:

- **What** do I want?
- **How much** does it cost?
- **When** do I want it?

## Break it into small steps

A big number can feel far away. So split it into weeks. Divide the price by the number of weeks.

For example, a $60 game in 8 weeks means $60 divided by 8. That is $7.50 each week. Now the goal feels close. Each week you only need to reach $7.50.

If you already have some money saved, take it off first. If you have $20 saved, you only need $40 more. Over 8 weeks, that is $5 each week.

## Keep it where you can see it

Write your goal on paper and put it near your piggy bank. Draw a jar and color in a part each week. Watching it fill up keeps you going.

## If something changes

Sometimes a week goes badly, and you save less. That is okay. You can save a little more next week, or give yourself a few more weeks. Goals can change. What matters is that you keep going, one small step at a time.

## Try it

Pick one thing you would like to save for. Write down what it is, how much it costs, and how many weeks you want to take. Divide the price by the weeks. That is your weekly saving target. Check it again in a week.
