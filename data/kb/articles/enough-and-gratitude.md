There is always something new to want. A new game, new shoes, a new gadget. Ads and friends can make it feel like you never have enough. Learning to notice what you already have is a powerful money skill.

## What is "enough"?

Enough means having what you need and some things that make you happy, without always chasing more. It does not mean never wanting anything. It means your happiness does not depend on the next thing you buy.

## The wanting treadmill

Have you ever wanted something so much, got it, and then soon wanted something else? Many people feel this. The happy feeling from a new thing often fades fast. If we keep chasing it, we keep spending, and never feel satisfied.

## Gratitude: noticing the good

**Gratitude** means being thankful. When you stop and notice the good things you already have, like friends, family, a favorite book, or a sunny day, you often feel happier. And you may feel less need to buy more.

## How it helps with money

- You spend more on what really matters to you.
- Saving gets easier, because you're not always chasing the next thing.
- You notice what others might need, which makes giving feel natural.

## Simple gratitude habits

- At dinner, each person shares one good thing from their day.
- Write down three things you're thankful for before bed.
- Say thank you, and mean it, when someone helps you.

## Try it

For one week, write down three things you are thankful for each day. Things that cost no money count. At the end of the week, read your list. How many items cost money? How many were free?
