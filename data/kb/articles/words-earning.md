People earn money in different ways, and each way has its own word. Here are some common ones, explained simply.

## Wage

A **wage** is pay for each hour worked. If you earn $12 an hour and work 10 hours, you earn $120.

## Salary

A **salary** is a set amount of pay for a year, split into regular paychecks, no matter the exact hours worked each week.

## Gross pay

**Gross pay** is the total you earned before anything is taken out.

## Net pay

**Net pay** is what you actually take home after taxes and other amounts are taken out. It is also called take-home pay.

## Tip

A **tip** is extra money a customer gives for good service, like at a restaurant. Some workers earn a big part of their pay from tips.

## Commission

A **commission** is pay based on how much someone sells, often a percent of each sale. A salesperson might earn 5% of every sale they make.

## Overtime

**Overtime** is work beyond the normal hours, often paid at a higher rate.

## Allowance

An **allowance** is money a grown-up gives a kid on a regular schedule. It is a great way to practice managing money.
