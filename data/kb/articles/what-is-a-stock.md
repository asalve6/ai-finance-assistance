A stock is a tiny slice of ownership in a company. If a company were a pizza, a stock would be one slice. People who own stock are called shareholders, and each slice is called a share.

## Why do companies sell stock?

Building a business takes money. A company might need money to open stores, build products, or hire workers. One way to raise money is to sell shares to the public. The company gets cash to grow, and the buyers get a small piece of the company.

## How do shareholders make money?

There are two main ways.

- **The price goes up.** If you buy a share and later sell it for more than you paid, you keep the difference.
- **Dividends.** Some companies share part of their profits with their shareholders. That payment is called a dividend.

## Prices can fall too

A share price can go down as well as up. If the company does poorly, or people lose faith in it, the price can drop, and you could get back less than you paid. This is why stocks are called risky. Nobody can know ahead of time which way a price will move.

## Where are stocks bought and sold?

Shares trade on a stock market. That is a place, often online, where buyers and sellers meet. The price of a share changes during the day as people buy and sell.

## Try it

Pick a company you know, like a game maker or a snack brand. Find out whether it sells stock to the public. If it does, look up its share price today and again in a week. Write down whether it moved, and one reason why it might have.
