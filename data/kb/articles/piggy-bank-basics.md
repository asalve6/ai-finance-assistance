A piggy bank is a simple tool with a big job. It keeps your money safe so you can use it later.

## Why save?

When you get money, you have a choice. You can spend it now, or you can save some for later. Saving means keeping money instead of spending it right away. Saved money can help you buy something bigger, like a game or a bike, that you could not afford with just one allowance.

## How a piggy bank works

Every time you put money in, it is called a deposit. Your pile grows a little at a time, and small deposits add up. If you put in five dollars a week, you will have twenty dollars after four weeks. After twelve weeks, you will have sixty dollars.

A piggy bank also gives you a pause. Because your money is tucked away, you have to stop and think before you take it out. That pause helps you avoid spending on things you will forget about next week.

## Saving with a goal

Saving is easier when you know what you are saving for. A goal is something you want to reach. Pick your goal and find out its price. Then divide the price by the number of weeks you are willing to wait.

Say a game costs forty dollars and you want it in eight weeks. Forty divided by eight is five, so you need to save five dollars each week. If you save a little more some weeks, you will get there even sooner.

## What if you slip?

Everyone spends some of their savings by accident sometimes. That is okay. Look at your goal again, work out the new weekly amount, and keep going. A small setback does not cancel your plan.

## Try it

Pick one thing you really want. Write down its price. Decide how many weeks you can wait, and work out how much to save each week. Then make your first deposit today, even if it is only a little.
