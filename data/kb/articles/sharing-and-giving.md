Money can do more than buy things for ourselves. It can also help other people. Sharing and giving are important ways to use money.

## Ways to give

- **Money.** Giving some of your money to a cause you care about, like an animal shelter or a food bank.
- **Time.** Helping at a community event or reading to younger kids.
- **Things.** Donating toys, clothes, or books you have outgrown.
- **Kindness.** Helping a neighbor carry groceries costs nothing and still matters.

## Plan your giving

Giving works best when you plan it, just like saving. Some kids set aside a small part of every allowance in a "give" jar. When the jar fills up, they choose where it goes.

## Choosing a cause

Think about what matters to you. Do you love animals? The environment? Helping kids who don't have enough food? A grown-up can help you find a trusted group that works on that cause.

It is smart to check that a charity is real before giving. Unfortunately, some people pretend to collect for charity as a scam. A grown-up can help you check.

## Why give?

Giving helps people and communities. It also feels good. Many families see giving as part of who they are, and pass the habit on.

## Giving is not about the amount

A small gift given with care matters. Giving $1 from your own allowance can mean more than a big gift that cost you nothing.

## Try it

Make a "give" jar and add a small amount each week for a month. With a grown-up, choose a cause you care about. Decide together how to give the money, and talk about what it will help do.
