Taxes are money that people and businesses pay to the government. The government uses that money to pay for things that everyone shares.

## What do taxes pay for?

Think about the things you use every day that you do not pay for at the door. Public schools, roads and bridges, parks, libraries, fire trucks, and police all depend on tax money. Taxes also help pay for people who are sick or out of work, and for keeping the country safe.

## Who pays taxes?

Most adults pay taxes, and so do businesses. There are a few main kinds.

- **Income tax:** a part of the money you earn from a job.
- **Sales tax:** a small amount added when you buy something in a store.
- **Property tax:** money paid by people who own land or buildings, which often helps pay for local schools.

You may have seen sales tax already. If a toy has a ten dollar price tag, the total at the register can be a bit higher because of the tax.

## Why do taxes matter to you?

When you get your first paycheck, it may be smaller than you expected. That is because some of the money was already sent to the government as tax. Knowing this ahead of time helps you plan how much you will really have to spend and save.

Tax rules differ from place to place and change over time. This article is for learning and is not tax advice.
