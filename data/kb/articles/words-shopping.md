Stores and apps use special words when you buy things. Here are some common ones, explained simply.

## Receipt

A **receipt** is a paper or email that shows what you bought, the price, the tax, and the date. Keep it in case you need to return something.

## Refund

A **refund** is money given back when you return something. Stores have their own rules about returns, so check before you buy.

## Discount

A **discount** is money taken off the usual price. "20% off" a $10 toy means you save $2 and pay $8, plus any tax.

## Coupon

A **coupon** is a ticket or code that gives you a discount. Only use one if you were already planning to buy that thing.

## Warranty

A **warranty** is a promise from the maker to fix or replace something if it breaks within a set time.

## Subscription

A **subscription** is a payment that repeats, like every month, for a service such as a game pass or a streaming app. It keeps charging until someone cancels it. Always ask a grown-up first.

## Free trial

A **free trial** lets you try something free for a short time. Many turn into paid subscriptions automatically if not canceled.

## Sales tax

**Sales tax** is a small extra amount added to the price when you pay.

## Try it

Look at a receipt at home. Find the price of one item, any discount, the tax, and the total. Then check together whether your family has any subscriptions, and how much they cost each month.
